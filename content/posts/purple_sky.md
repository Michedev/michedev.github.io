My painting depicts a person standing alone on a beach at night, gazing out at the luminous blue ocean and contemplating on the otherworldly colors above.

#### The Purple Night

Under a purple sky, stars glimmering bright,     
A solitary figure stands, absorbed in the night.    
The ocean's blue hue, a mirror of the scene,    
Reflects the cosmic wonder, ethereal and serene.    

In moments unexpected, life's colors may change.    

The purple night's lesson,     
profound and true,    
Is to embrace life's twists,    
In the face of the unforeseen,     
we stand and reflect,    
pondering the challenges,     
that life may project.    

When faced with the surprising,     
we may feel perplexed,    
but these are the moments,     
where our strengths are flexed.    
In navigating the unfamiliar,     
we discover our might,    
and illuminate our path,     
like stars in the night.
